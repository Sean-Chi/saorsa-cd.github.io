Story & Background

At the centre of the galaxy lies a mysterious signal, pulling travellers toward it. This is the core story thread of No Man’s Sky. Players will uncover secrets of the Atlas, ancient alien races, and the meaning behind this simulated universe.

Since release, Hello Games has released dozens of free major updates. Multiplayer, base building, freighter fleets, expeditions and VR support have all been added. The game keeps growing and changing after years of support.

Play Styles

  • Explorer: discover new species and uncharted worlds
  • Trader: harvest rare resources and trade across star systems
  • Fighter: battle space pirates and hostile Sentinels
  • Builder: build bases on planets, freighters and space platforms
