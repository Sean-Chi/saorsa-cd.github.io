Core Game Features

Seamless Space Flight

Take off from a planet’s surface and fly directly into orbit. Warp between star systems using hyperdrive. There are no loading barriers between ground and space.

Unique Ships & Tools

Collect fighter ships, explorer shuttles, large freighters and living organic ships. Upgrade your multi-tool, exosuit and jetpack to survive extreme environments.

Base Building & Farming

Build outposts anywhere: on planets, underwater, or inside asteroid stations. Grow crops, hire NPC workers and automate resource production.

Multiplayer & Expeditions

Join friends at the Space Anomaly, complete timed expedition events, race exocraft or fight pirates together.
